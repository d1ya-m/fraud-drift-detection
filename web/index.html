Key Experimental Takeaways

01
Models Die Silently Without Maintenance: When fraud patterns shift, static models don't trigger internal alerts; after drift, the frozen model raised just 1 alert across 134,807 transactions and caught 0 frauds.
02
Standard Evaluation Deceives: A standard shuffled test split reported 0.888 PR-AUC, but the deployed model scored 0.069 after drift. Standard cross-validation completely conceals production decay.
03
Periodic Retraining Outperforms Detectors: Retraining on recent data every 3 batches was the most reliable strategy, beating reactive drift detectors by eliminating detection lag.
04
Why Drift Detectors Lag: Because fraud labels are rare (~13 per 10k batch), statistical detectors require 1 to 9 batches to accumulate enough misses to alarm.
